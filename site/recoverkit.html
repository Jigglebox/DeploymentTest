Setup

  1. Create a restricted Stripe key: Invoices write, Customers read, Billing portal write.
  2. Add a webhook for invoice.payment_failed, invoice.paid, customer.subscription.deleted, payment_method.attached.
  3. Add a sending domain. Emails go out from your billing address, in your name.

The campaign

  1. Day 0 — "Your payment didn't go through" with a one-click card-update link. Access continues.
  2. Day 3 — retry at 10:00 UTC, reminder email.
  3. Day 7 — retry, "subscription at risk".
  4. Day 14 — retry, final notice.
  5. Day 21 — case closed as lost; nothing further is sent.
